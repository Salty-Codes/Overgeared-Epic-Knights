Overgeared x Epic Knights Changelog
=========================

1.2.0 (2026-09-18)
------------------
- Armour no longer needs or accepts a blueprint to forge — Overgeared itself doesn't
  gate armour on blueprints either, only weapons and shields keep theirs
- Verified against Overgeared 1.20.1-1.6.34.1 and 1.21.1-1.6.18.1; minimum Overgeared
  version raised accordingly
- Rebased the 1.21.1-neoforge Epic Knights dependency onto official 10.15 (from a locally
  patched 10.12): the DataGen crash guard was reapplied to the new jar, the crusader
  armour colour fix we carried is no longer needed since upstream now has it

1.1.0 (2026-09-08)
------------------
- Epic Knights: Addon support (optional): forging, casting, assembly, blueprints and
  tool types for every addon steel weapon, anvil forging for every armour piece and decoration
  with metal to hammer, and quality-preserving assembly for parts joined by hand onto forged
  armour; recipes live in a built-in datapack that is only enabled when the addon is installed
- The chivalry lance is crafted from plates instead of ingots, like the shields
- Forging for the base mod's brigandine, platemail boots, XIV century knight boots and the
  ecranche, rondel, spike and two-plumes decorations (stock metal with leather or cloth fitted)
- Recipe removal is now rule-based: every Epic Knights recipe whose result we forge is
  dropped automatically
- Names, armour recipes and tool-type lists are generated from one definition table
- New tooling under tools/ to derive blade textures and addon recipes, and to check
  for recipe conflicts (all base and addon recipes are verified conflict-free)
- Verified against Overgeared 1.20.1-1.6.33 and 1.21.1-1.6.17; minimum Overgeared version raised
  accordingly
- The Epic Knights blacksmith hammer is tagged as an Overgeared smithing hammer (iron tier)
  and works on the smithing anvil

1.0.0 (2026)
------------
- Initial release
- Compatibility recipes for all Epic Knights weapon types using Overgeared materials:
  forging, casting, and assembly
- Supported materials: Iron, Gold, Bronze, Copper, Tin, Steel, Silver, Stone
- Supported weapon types: Claymore, Zweihander, Lochaberaxe, Guisarme, Ahlspiess,
  Lucernhammer, Heavymace, Heavy Crossbow, and more
- Armor set compatibility: Kastenbrust, Burgonet, Cuirass, and additional sets
- Removes default vanilla crafting recipes for Epic Knights items where replaced
  by Overgeared recipes
