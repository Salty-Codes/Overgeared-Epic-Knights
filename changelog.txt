Overgeared x Epic Knights Changelog
=========================

1.2.0 (2026-11-05)
------------------
- Epic Knights: Slavic Armory, Antique Legacy and Dark Ages support (all optional, each with its
  own built-in datapack that is only enabled when the addon is installed): forging, casting,
  assembly, blueprints and tool types for all 45 addon weapons, and anvil forging for every
  armour piece, decoration and shield with metal to hammer
- Epic Knights: Ores and Alloys support on 1.20.1 (optional, there is no NeoForge build): bronze,
  silver and tin tools are forged and cast as heads and assembled like Overgeared's own, its steel
  recipes and its copper and steel tools are removed in favour of Overgeared's. Its silver also
  makes our silver gear obtainable. With Antique Legacy installed too, Ores and Alloys' tin, bronze
  and copper nuggets take over: Antique Legacy's recipes, tags and ore generation for them are
  gone, its items stay in the game like Epic Knights' steel
- Antique Legacy's bronze and iron plates now come off the anvil: its plate recipes are replaced
  by our bronze plate and Overgeared's iron plate, its small plates are forged
- Pieces of bronze alone only need a stone anvil, like the bronze blades
- Forged pieces keep their quality through every step: the alla tedesca chestplate, greenwich
  boots, saracen helmet, kuyak chestplate and hairy boar decorations are assembled on their forged
  base instead of crafted, as is the flamebladed sword on 1.20.1
- The kuyak chestplate now takes two steps: forge the breastplate, then join the mail by hand
- Slavic Armory's forged steel gear blasts into Overgeared steel nuggets
- Fixed the forged steel blasting recipe failing to load on 1.21.1
- The pitchfork and all rusted gear blast into steel nuggets only, some of them could still give
  iron nuggets
- Every weapon with a casting recipe can now really be cast: only a few tool types had a cast
  capacity, the casts of all others held no metal
- Stone blades work as templates for tool casts, like Overgeared's stone heads
- The katzbalger, lochaber axe, heavy mace, heavy crossbow prodd, francisca axe, war hammer, pike,
  dagger, bollock dagger, short spear and antique spear are forged from plates where the part is
  flat: in plain ingots they took the place of Overgeared's tool heads when forging without a
  blueprint
- Fixed the stone guisarme blade: its recipe mirrored the stone claymore blade's, so only one of
  them could be crafted. Cobblestone now goes below the blade
- The forging tab moves to the front in EMI and right behind crafting in JEI. For JEI this edits
  its recipe-category-sort-order.ini once per installation, later changes there are kept
- Verified against Overgeared 1.20.1-1.6.37 and 1.21.1-1.6.20 and Epic Knights 10.15 (1.20.1) and
  10.17 (1.21.1); minimum versions raised accordingly

1.1.1 (2026-09-18)
------------------
- Armour no longer needs or accepts a blueprint to forge — Overgeared itself doesn't
  gate armour on blueprints either, only weapons and shields keep theirs
- Verified against Overgeared 1.20.1-1.6.34.1 and 1.21.1-1.6.18.1; minimum Overgeared
  version raised accordingly
- Rebased the 1.21.1-neoforge Epic Knights dependency onto official 10.15 (from a locally
  patched 10.12): the DataGen crash guard was reapplied to the new jar, the crusader
  armour colour fix we carried is no longer needed since upstream now has it

1.1.0 (2026-09-08)
------------------
- Epic Knights: Addon support (optional): forging, casting, assembly, blueprints and
  tool types for every addon steel weapon, anvil forging for every armour piece and decoration
  with metal to hammer, and quality-preserving assembly for parts joined by hand onto forged
  armour; recipes live in a built-in datapack that is only enabled when the addon is installed
- The chivalry lance is crafted from plates instead of ingots, like the shields
- Forging for the base mod's brigandine, platemail boots, XIV century knight boots and the
  ecranche, rondel, spike and two-plumes decorations (stock metal with leather or cloth fitted)
- Recipe removal is now rule-based: every Epic Knights recipe whose result we forge is
  dropped automatically
- Names, armour recipes and tool-type lists are generated from one definition table
- New tooling under tools/ to derive blade textures and addon recipes, and to check
  for recipe conflicts (all base and addon recipes are verified conflict-free)
- Verified against Overgeared 1.20.1-1.6.33 and 1.21.1-1.6.17; minimum Overgeared version raised
  accordingly
- The Epic Knights blacksmith hammer is tagged as an Overgeared smithing hammer (iron tier)
  and works on the smithing anvil

1.0.0 (2026)
------------
- Initial release
- Compatibility recipes for all Epic Knights weapon types using Overgeared materials:
  forging, casting, and assembly
- Supported materials: Iron, Gold, Bronze, Copper, Tin, Steel, Silver, Stone
- Supported weapon types: Claymore, Zweihander, Lochaberaxe, Guisarme, Ahlspiess,
  Lucernhammer, Heavymace, Heavy Crossbow, and more
- Armor set compatibility: Kastenbrust, Burgonet, Cuirass, and additional sets
- Removes default vanilla crafting recipes for Epic Knights items where replaced
  by Overgeared recipes
